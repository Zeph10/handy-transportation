# Driver guide: sharing your availability

Use this website to tell Handy Transportation when you can drive. **It's optional**: share what you like, when you like. It works on your phone.

## 1. Log in

1. Open the website and enter your email. The first time, add your name too.
2. We email you a link. Tap it on the same phone or computer. (No password.)
   - Opened the email on another device? Type the 6-digit code from the email on the login page instead.
3. New accounts wait for the dispatcher to approve them. You'll be able to log in fully once approved.

## 2. First time: three quick steps

1. **Vehicles**: add the vehicle you drive and how many passengers and large bags it holds.
2. **Destinations & rates**: tick the trips you take (EWR, LGA, JFK, NYC, Other) and your minimum pay for each. The company standard rate is filled in for you; change it if you like.
3. **Weekly schedule** (optional): if you usually drive the same times each week, add them once and they repeat.

## 3. Add availability

On **My availability**:

- Tap **+ Add availability**, pick the date and times, and tap **Save**. Your usual vehicle, destinations and rates are already filled in.
- On a computer you can also drag across the calendar.
- Choose **Available**, **Tentative** (maybe) or **Unavailable**.
- Need a different rate or vehicle for one block? Change it in that block only.
- Overnight is fine: 8 PM to 4 AM shows across two days but stays one entry.

Anything you haven't filled in shows to the dispatcher as **unknown**, not available.

## 4. Shortcuts

- **Copy yesterday** or **Copy last week** in one tap.
- **Copy to other days** from any block (pick the days).
- **Templates**: save a week you like and reuse it.
- **Clear day/week**: removes one-off blocks; you choose whether to also skip your weekly schedule for those dates.

## 5. Time off

Add vacation or days off on **Time off**. Time off overrides your weekly schedule.

## 6. Short-notice changes

If you remove or shorten availability very close to the time (the default is within 24 hours), the change goes to the dispatcher for approval. Your original availability stays until they approve. Adding more time always saves straight away.

## 7. If the dispatcher changes something

You'll see a note on your calendar and the details under **Changes & requests**, including the reason.

## Reminders

You may get a weekly email reminder to share next week's availability. It's a reminder, not a requirement. Turn it off under **Profile**.
